    A napkin sketch or an idea.

  • A product with a problem

Our clients ask for

  • A prototype that proves the concept is feasible.

  • Engineering for one subsystem: a board, the firmware, the enclosure.

  • The full product, through to the first production run.

  • A design review, or the root cause of a failure.

Engineering

  • Electronics

    Architecture, component selection, schematic capture, multilayer PCB layout, bring-up and test.

  • Embedded software

    Firmware for microcontrollers and embedded processors: drivers, communication, power management and field updates.

  • Mechanical design

    Enclosures and mechanisms, tolerances, material and process selection.

  • Design for manufacture

    Parts and assemblies designed for the process that makes them, with the supply chain in mind from the start.

  • Software and cloud

    Device back ends, data pipelines and the applications around the product.

Production

  • Rapid prototyping

    Functional prototypes for test and review.

  • Small batch manufacture

    The first units for your customers.

  • Production handover

    Sourcing, and a production-ready package for your contract manufacturer.

How a project runs

  1. Scope

    We review what you have and send a technical plan and a quote.

  2. Engineer

    Short iterations, with hardware on your desk at each one.

  3. Validate

    Bring-up, test and a DFM review before anything goes to production.

  4. Produce

    We build the batch, or hand a production-ready package to your manufacturer.

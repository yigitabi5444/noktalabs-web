How a project runs

  1. Scope

    We go through what you have and send a technical plan and a quote.

  2. Engineer

    Short iterations, with hardware on your desk at each one.

  3. Validate

    Bring-up, testing and a manufacturability check before anything goes to production.

  4. Produce

    We build the batch, or hand a production-ready package to your manufacturer.
